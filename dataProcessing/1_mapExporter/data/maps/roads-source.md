# Natural Earth Roads

Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/roads/
Download: https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/10m_cultural/ne_10m_roads.zip
Version: 5.0.0. Natural Earth data are public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Converted to gzip-compressed GeoJSON. Ferry features and tracks omitted; coordinates rounded to five decimal places. Only road type and scale rank retained. Global coverage, generalized geometry; not a complete city street network.
